September 28, 2026. This is a preparation sheet, not submitted App Store metadata. Confirm the final brand, pricing and reviewed tool scope before copying it into App Store Connect.

## Proposed listing

Working name: **Electrician Toolbox**. Name availability and trademark clearance have not been established.

Suggested subtitle: **Everyday jobsite tools**

Suggested category: Utilities. Initial territory: United States, as approved by the owner. Both are configured in App Store Connect, not automatically by the app's language or bundle identifier.

Description draft:

Electrician Toolbox keeps everyday jobsite calculations, circuit color references and material lists together on your iPhone. Its core tools work offline after installation, with no required app account.

Use Workpad for feet, inches and fractions. Correct an equation directly, choose measurement precision and revisit saved calculations. Panel Colors matches circuit numbers to the color scheme you select, with nearby circuits and saved job presets.

Keep a separate Jobsite List for each job, track remaining material quantities and leave notes about where work stopped. Use Fill Guide for supported conduit and box-fill checks, Wire Guide for supported conductor ampacity calculations, Trade Talk for electrical terms and jobsite slang, and the Bending Suite for illustrated hand-bender layouts.

Choose a light or dark theme across the app. Export a manual backup of saved work and restore it later. There is no automatic cross-device sync; keep backup files somewhere you trust.

Electrical results are calculation aids, not inspection approval or a complete installation design. Verify the adopted code, equipment ratings, panel identification, manufacturer instructions and actual conditions. Color conventions vary. Bending diagrams are schematic and tool settings must match your bender.

Before publication: revise this text to match the final reviewed feature scope and actual paid/free access. Do not advertise future AI, automatic compliance, universal bender deductions, guaranteed safe wire sizing, cloud backup, or any unimplemented subscription.
